# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the business capability responsible for managing organizational notifications within MachineryManagerEnterprise.

The Notification Center distributes business information generated by other capabilities to the appropriate recipients.

It ensures that important business events reach the correct people at the correct time through appropriate communication channels.

Notification Center communicates business events.

It does not create business events.

It does not execute business operations.

It does not make business decisions.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Modern maintenance organizations generate a large number of important business events every day.

Examples include:

- Critical equipment failures
- Maintenance forecasts
- Scheduled maintenance reminders
- Work Order assignments
- Component replacements
- Safety alerts
- Warranty expirations
- Inventory shortages

Without centralized notification management:

- important events may be missed;
- maintenance activities may be delayed;
- safety risks may increase;
- organizational coordination becomes inconsistent;
- accountability becomes difficult;
- business response time deteriorates.

The organization therefore requires a centralized capability for managing business notifications independently from the business processes that generate them.

---

# 3. Business Goals

The platform shall enable the organization to:

- distribute business information consistently;
- notify the appropriate recipients;
- prioritize important business events;
- support multiple delivery channels;
- preserve notification history;
- improve operational responsiveness;
- reduce communication delays;
- support business accountability;
- provide complete notification traceability.

Notification Center improves business communication.

It does not replace business processes.

---

# 4. Scope

Notification Center is responsible for managing business notifications after business events have occurred.

The capability begins when another business capability produces a notification-worthy business event.

The capability ends when notification delivery has been completed, cancelled, expired or archived.

Notification Center manages communication.

It does not generate business events.

# 7. Notification Lifecycle

## Business Definition

Every Business Notification progresses through its own communication lifecycle.

The Notification Lifecycle represents the communication process rather than the lifecycle of the originating business event.

A Notification may complete its lifecycle while the originating business object continues to evolve.

Notification history shall always remain independent.

---

## Standard Lifecycle

The standard Notification lifecycle is illustrated below.

```text
Created

↓

Queued

↓

Delivered

↓

Viewed

↓

Acknowledged

↓

Archived

or

Cancelled
```

Organizations may extend the lifecycle while preserving complete communication history.

---

## Lifecycle States

### Created

A Notification has been generated.

Generation occurs after a business event has satisfied notification rules.

Examples:

- Incident Created
- Forecast Approved
- Maintenance Completed
- Work Order Assigned

Creation does not imply delivery.

---

### Queued

The Notification has been accepted for delivery.

Recipient resolution has completed.

Delivery channel has been selected.

The Notification is waiting for transmission.

---

### Delivered

The Notification has successfully reached its intended delivery channel.

Examples:

- Inbox
- Mobile Application
- Email
- SMS
- Dashboard

Delivery confirms transmission.

It does not confirm that the recipient has seen the message.

---

### Viewed

The recipient has opened or viewed the Notification.

Viewing records:

- Recipient
- View Time
- Viewing Device (when available)

Viewing does not imply business acceptance.

---

### Acknowledged

The recipient explicitly confirms awareness.

Acknowledgement records:

- Recipient
- Timestamp
- Optional Business Note

Acknowledgement satisfies business communication requirements.

---

### Archived

The Notification has completed its communication purpose.

Archived Notifications remain available for historical reporting.

Archiving shall never remove communication history.

---

### Cancelled

The Notification becomes unnecessary before completion.

Examples:

- Duplicate Notification
- Originating Event Withdrawn
- Business Decision Changed

Cancellation shall preserve the business reason.

---

## Reminder Cycle

Some Notifications may require repeated reminders.

Example

```text
Created

↓

Delivered

↓

Reminder

↓

Reminder

↓

Acknowledged
```

Every Reminder shall preserve its own delivery history.

---

## Escalation Cycle

Notifications requiring acknowledgement may escalate.

Example

```text
Created

↓

Delivered

↓

No Response

↓

Escalation

↓

Manager Notification
```

Escalation generates new Notifications.

The original Notification remains unchanged.

---

## Lifecycle Business Rules

### BR-NL-001

Every Notification shall begin in the Created state.

---

### BR-NL-002

Only Queued Notifications may be delivered.

---

### BR-NL-003

Delivery shall never imply recipient awareness.

Only Viewed or Acknowledged states confirm recipient interaction.

---

### BR-NL-004

Every lifecycle transition shall preserve:

- Timestamp
- Previous State
- New State
- Responsible Actor (if applicable)
- Business Reason

---

### BR-NL-005

Archived Notifications remain permanent historical records.

Historical communication shall never be deleted.

---

### BR-NL-006

Reminder and Escalation activities shall never modify previous delivery history.

Each Reminder and Escalation creates additional communication history.

---

## Business Outcomes

Notification Lifecycle enables:

- reliable communication;
- communication auditing;
- SLA monitoring;
- acknowledgement tracking;
- escalation management;
- organizational accountability.

# 11. Notification Rules

## Business Definition

Business Notifications shall be generated only when business rules determine that communication is required.

Business events do not automatically create notifications.

Notification generation is governed by explicit business policies.

---

## Notification Trigger

A Notification may be generated when:

- a business event occurs;
- notification policy permits communication;
- at least one recipient can be resolved;
- delivery conditions are satisfied.

Otherwise, no Notification shall be created.

---

## Event Qualification

Only business events explicitly designated as notification-worthy may generate Notifications.

Examples include:

- Incident Created
- Critical Incident Escalated
- Maintenance Forecast Generated
- Work Order Assigned
- Maintenance Completed
- Asset Status Changed
- Warranty Expiration
- Approval Requested

Routine internal processing shall not automatically generate Notifications.

---

## Duplicate Prevention

Repeated occurrence of the same business event shall not necessarily create duplicate Notifications.

Business policy may suppress repeated communication.

Example

```text
Hydraulic Pressure Low

↓

Notification Created

↓

Pressure Still Low

↓

No Duplicate Notification
```

Until business conditions change.

---

## Notification Frequency

Business policies may control communication frequency.

Examples:

- Once
- Daily
- Hourly
- Every Status Change
- Every Escalation Level

Frequency shall remain configurable.

---

## Priority Rules

Business Priority influences notification behavior.

Typical priorities include:

- Informational
- Normal
- Important
- Critical
- Emergency

Priority affects:

- Delivery Channels
- Reminder Policy
- Escalation
- Recipient Hierarchy

---

## Quiet Hours

Organizations may define communication restrictions.

Examples:

- Night Hours
- Holidays
- Weekends

Critical Notifications may bypass these restrictions.

Business policy determines exceptions.

---

## Escalation Rules

Escalation shall occur only when business conditions require it.

Typical escalation conditions:

- Notification not acknowledged
- SLA exceeded
- Critical Incident unresolved
- Required Approval delayed

Escalation produces additional Notifications.

---

## Reminder Rules

Examples:

↓

Reminder

↓

Reminder

↓

Reminder sequence terminates immediately after business objective has been achieved.

---

## Notification Cancellation

Notifications may be cancelled before delivery.

Examples:

- Business Event Withdrawn
- Duplicate Notification
- Invalid Recipient
- Business Decision Changed

Cancellation shall preserve complete historical traceability.

---

## Business Rules

Notifications shall only be generated from authorized business events.

### BR-NR-002

Notification policy determines communication.

---

### BR-NR-003

---

### BR-NR-004

# 12. Business Constraints

## Business Definition

Notification Center is a supporting business capability.

Its responsibility is limited to business communication.

Notification Center shall never alter the business state of the originating business objects.

Business ownership always remains with the originating capability.

---

## Event Integrity

Notification Center consumes Business Events.

It shall never:

- create business events;
- modify business events;
- delete business events;
- delay business event processing.

Business Events remain immutable after publication.

---

## Source Integrity

Notification Center shall never modify:

- Incident records;
- Maintenance Forecasts;
- Work Orders;
- Maintenance Operations;
- Assets;
- Tracked Components;
- Parts;
- Business Relationships.

All source business objects remain owned by their respective business capabilities.

---

## Communication Integrity

Business Notifications represent communication history.

Communication history shall never be:

- overwritten;
- deleted;
- replaced;
- reassigned.

Historical communication shall remain permanently reproducible.

---

## Recipient Integrity

Recipient Resolution shall remain read-only.

Notification Center shall never:

- assign personnel;
- modify organizational hierarchy;
- change business responsibilities;
- create relationships.

Recipient Resolution consumes organizational information.

It never owns it.

---

## Delivery Integrity

Delivery attempts shall never modify the originating Notification.

Examples

```text
Delivery Failed

↓

Retry
```

Retry creates additional delivery history.

The original delivery record remains unchanged.

---

## Acknowledgement Integrity

Acknowledgement represents recipient interaction.

Acknowledgement shall never modify:

- originating business events;
- originating Notifications;
- delivery history.

Acknowledgement creates new historical records.

---

## Escalation Integrity

Escalation produces additional Notifications.

Escalation shall never:

- replace previous Notifications;
- modify delivery history;
- remove recipients.

Escalation extends communication history.

---

## Notification Lifecycle Integrity

Notification lifecycle remains independent from the lifecycle of the originating business object.

Example

```text
Maintenance Completed

↓

Notification Delivered

↓

Notification Archived
```

Maintenance lifecycle remains unchanged.

- Notification Creation

- Acknowledgement History

Corrections create additional historical records.

Historical truth shall never be modified.

---

## Business Constraints

The platform shall prevent:

- duplicate communication history;
- orphan Notifications;
- missing source references;
- invalid recipients;
- inconsistent lifecycle transitions;
- unauthorized delivery;
- historical modification.

---

---

Notification processing shall never modify the originating business object.

### BR-NC-003

Notification Center shall remain independent from organizational ownership.

### BR-NC-005

### BR-NC-006

## Business Outcomes

Business Constraints ensure:

- immutable communication history;

# 15. Architectural Position

Notification Center is a supporting domain capability.

It connects business events with organizational communication.

Notification Center never owns business processes.

Instead it communicates business state changes.

```text
Business Event

↓

Recipient Resolution

↓

Notification

↓

Delivery

↓

Acknowledgement

↓

Communication History
```

## Responsibilities

Notification Center owns:

- Notification Lifecycle
- Delivery History
- Reminder History
- Escalation History
- Communication Audit
- Recipient Resolution Process

Notification Center does not own:

- Business Events
- Organizational Structure
- Asset State
- Maintenance Operations
- Forecasts
- Incidents
- Parts
- Tracked Components
